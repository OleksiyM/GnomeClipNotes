# Code of Conduct

GnomeClipNotes welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Help make participation respectful, useful and safe.

## How we work together

- Be considerate. Critique ideas, code and behavior, not someone's identity or worth.
- Disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome; personal attacks,
  harassment, threats and discriminatory or sexualized abuse are not.
- Respect boundaries. Stop unwanted contact, and do not publish another person's
  private information without permission.
- Protect privacy in reports: redact clipboard contents, notes, credentials and
  personal information from examples, logs and screenshots.
- Give people room to ask questions, make mistakes and correct them.

These expectations apply in the project's issues, pull requests and other
project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, OleksiyM, at
[oleksiymaliovanyy@gmail.com](mailto:oleksiymaliovanyy@gmail.com). Include links
and enough context to understand what happened; do not post sensitive details
in a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly, with information shared
only as needed to assess and address the concern.

The maintainer may ask for a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content,
close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently,
depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. Good-faith reports
must not lead to retaliation. If you believe a decision was mistaken, explain
your concerns through the same private contact.

This is a small independently maintained project, without a dedicated moderation
team or guaranteed response time. Security vulnerabilities have a separate
[private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
